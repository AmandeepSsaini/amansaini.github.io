01 — Who I am

Engineer first.
Architect by necessity.
Founder by choice.

I've spent a decade at the seam between infrastructure and intelligence. Started on backend platforms, moved into ML systems when models stopped being a research artifact, and now spend my days on the hardest version of that problem: getting many agents to cooperate reliably, under budget, at latency people will tolerate.

As a Staff engineer I'm the person teams call when a system is technically correct and still failing. As a Tech Lead I care more about the six other engineers shipping well than about my own diff. As a Co-Founder I learned that architecture is a business decision wearing a lab coat.

Right now I build agent orchestration runtimes — planning loops, tool routing, evaluation harnesses, memory — and I advise teams trying to get their first agent system past the demo.
