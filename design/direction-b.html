Engineer first.
Architect by necessity.
Founder by choice.

I've spent a decade at the seam between infrastructure and intelligence. I started on backend platforms, moved into ML systems when models stopped being a research artifact, and now spend my days on the hardest version of that problem — getting many agents to cooperate reliably, under budget, at a latency people will actually tolerate.

As a Staff engineer I'm the person teams call when a system is technically correct and still failing. As a Tech Lead I care more about six other engineers shipping well than about my own diff. As a Co-Founder I learned that architecture is a business decision wearing a lab coat.

These days I build agent orchestration runtimes — planning loops, tool routing, evaluation harnesses, memory — and I advise teams trying to get their first agent system past the demo and into something they can put a customer in front of.
